# Code of Conduct

matra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, agrees to it.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and criticise ideas, not people. Many reports here are about languages and scripts that the reader may not know. Ask, do not assume.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code, email the maintainer at pulkit.enfpa@gmail.com. Reports are private. The maintainer replies within a week and decides what to do, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
